## Section 2 — How we work

```html
<section class="kd-wp">
  <span class="kd-eyebrow">How we work</span>
  <h2 class="kd-wp__title">Reliable decision-making bases – <em>legally sound and traceable.</em></h2>
  <p class="kd-wp__meta">Method · Kanduit, Düsseldorf</p>
  <p class="kd-wp__dek">
    The public sector in NRW rarely needs more data. It needs solutions that quickly turn
    existing data into a robust, legally sound basis for decisions.
  </p>

  <div class="kd-abstract">
    <span class="kd-ablabel">In brief</span>
    <p>
      Most of the data a municipality or school needs already exists – it accumulates in
      day-to-day administration: applications, asset records, case files, status reports. The
      bottleneck is not collection, but the effort of turning that record into something
      reliable. We rely on lean, explainable systems, data sovereignty without vendor lock-in,
      and results your departments can use right away.
    </p>
  </div>

  <article class="kd-sec">
    <div class="kd-sec__num">§ 01</div>
    <div>
      <h3 class="kd-sec__h">Make existing data usable</h3>
      <p class="kd-sec__p">
        Operational data is produced in administration anyway, yet often goes unused. We
        consolidate it, prepare it and bring it together into a single source of truth – with
        no new surveys and no data silos.
      </p>
    </div>
  </article>

  <article class="kd-sec">
    <div class="kd-sec__num">§ 02</div>
    <div>
      <h3 class="kd-sec__h">Legal certainty as an engine for innovation</h3>
      <p class="kd-sec__p">
        Fear of legal mistakes or security gaps is often the biggest brake on innovation. We
        build GDPR compliance and security-by-design into every project from the start –
        legally sound and audit-ready, rather than retrofitted.
      </p>
    </div>
  </article>

  <article class="kd-sec">
    <div class="kd-sec__num">§ 03</div>
    <div>
      <h3 class="kd-sec__h">Sovereign, not dependent</h3>
      <p class="kd-sec__p">
        Technology must stay under your control. Where possible we use sovereign open-source
        alternatives and open data standards – so you never end up dependent on a single vendor.
      </p>
    </div>
  </article>

  <div class="kd-note">
    <b>Note</b> Every analysis is documented and traceable. Personal data is avoided in a
    GDPR-compliant way and small counts are suppressed. Where we work with assumptions, we
    state them transparently.
  </div>
</section>
```
